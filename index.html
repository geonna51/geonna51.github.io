Amin - Stock Tracking iOS App

Amin is an iOS application built to help users track and manage stocks of interest. The app merges several ideas into one platform, allowing users to pick specific stocks, explore pre-built portfolios such as high-risk and safe options, and view simplified investment ideas designed to be accessible across different countries. The focus is on making stock tracking intuitive and approachable for new investors.

Technologies Used:
  • Swift & SwiftUI
  • Core Data
  • Alamofire
  • Charts Library
  • Firebase Authentication
Key Features:
  • Customizable watchlists
  • Portfolio exploration by risk level
  • Real-time stock data
  • Investment recommendations
  • Cross-device sync
